## Desk 3.7.1

**Windows stay out from under the bar**

- DeskBar: a window dropped so low that only a sliver of its top showed above the bar (the rest hidden under the bar and off the screen) is now lifted back up, at the same size, until it sits on the bar.
- DeskBar: tapping the bar with a finger right on its top edge no longer starts a resize of a snapped or normal window sitting on the bar. The tap goes to the bar instead. Maximized windows already worked this way; resizing with the mouse is unchanged.
- DeskDrawer and DeskTune are the same as 3.7.

**What you need:** Microsoft's free [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64)](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0) for DeskDrawer and DeskTune; DeskBar needs nothing extra. With 2.2 or later installed, each app offers this update itself. The files aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen asks the first time you run a downloaded setup: More info, then Run anyway. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
