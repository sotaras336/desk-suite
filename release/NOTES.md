## Desk 3.8

**A full-spectrum colour picker**

- DeskBar and DeskDrawer: the colour bar is now a colour pad. Every hue runs across, and the colour goes from vivid at the top to grey at the bottom. A light bar beside it runs from white, through your colour, to black, so dim, dusky and grey colours work, and the ends of the bar are the White and Black themes.
- All three apps read the new colours (`custom:<hue>:<fade>:<vivid>:<light>`); colours you already saved look the same.

**What you need:** Microsoft's free [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64)](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0) for DeskDrawer and DeskTune; DeskBar needs nothing extra. With 2.2 or later installed, each app offers this update itself. The files aren't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen asks the first time you run a downloaded setup: More info, then Run anyway. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
